# Legal & Compliance Knowledge Base — Agrolt Solutions

## Source hierarchy
1. Government notification/circular with an effective date.
2. Applicable Central/State rules.
3. Parent Labour Code / Act.
4. Internal company policy, only for company-specific process.

The agent must never treat an enacted Code as automatically commenced. It must check commencement/effective-date language in the available sources and flag when the corpus does not establish commencement.

## Core source facts

### Code on Wages
The uploaded Gazette states that the Code on Wages, 2019 consolidates laws relating to wages and bonus and provides that commencement is by Central Government notification. Source: COW-2019, section 1. fileciteturn482file1L2-L15

Gujarat Rules 2021 state that they extend to Gujarat and come into force from commencement of the Code on Wages. The rules also prescribe wage records/registers and wage slips; the retrieved text specifies Form I/Form V and wage-slip issuance within five working days of payment. fileciteturn482file5L35-L43 fileciteturn483file0

The Gujarat wage rules describe a normal working day as eight hours with rest intervals totaling not more than one hour and a spread-over limit of twelve hours, while preserving the application of other applicable safety/shop/factory provisions. fileciteturn484file5

### Industrial Relations
The Industrial Relations Code, 2020 consolidates trade-union, employment-condition and industrial-dispute laws; its commencement is also by notification. fileciteturn482file2L2-L18

The Gujarat Industrial Relations Rules 2021 apply throughout Gujarat where Gujarat is the appropriate Government and include Works Committee procedures. fileciteturn482file6L15-L28

The Gujarat IR forms contain specific procedures/timelines for retrenchment, closure and lay-off applications/intimations. The retrieved Form IX text, for example, specifies service periods for lay-off, retrenchment and closure applications; the agent must verify establishment-specific applicability before advising. fileciteturn483file6

### Social Security
The Social Security Code consolidates social-security laws. Its commencement is by notification and may differ by provision. fileciteturn482file3L2-L18

The uploaded Code's First Schedule states applicability thresholds including EPF at establishments with 20 or more employees, ESI at establishments with 10 or more persons subject to the stated conditions, and gratuity/maternity coverage for factories and specified establishments. The agent must quote the schedule and applicable notifications rather than infer eligibility solely from headcount. fileciteturn484file3

The Code also contains record/register/return obligations including employee records, attendance, hours, wages, leave, overtime, statutory deductions and wage slips. fileciteturn483file9

The Code contains maternity-benefit provisions, including payment timing requirements in the retrieved text. fileciteturn484file8

### Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions
The OSH Code consolidates occupational safety, health and working-condition laws; commencement is by notification. fileciteturn482file4L2-L18

The Code places responsibility on the employer for prescribed health, safety and working conditions and identifies matters such as cleanliness, ventilation, drinking water, lighting, sanitation and waste/effluent arrangements. fileciteturn484file9

It also contains safety-committee/safety-officer provisions and welfare thresholds, including canteen provisions for establishments with 100 or more workers including contract labourers, subject to the Code and applicable rules. fileciteturn484file0

## Mandatory answer policy
- Never invent a section, rule, form, rate, threshold, deadline or penalty.
- Always identify the source document and legal provision.
- State whether the answer is: Source-confirmed, Source-partial, or Requires-current-notification verification.
- Separate statutory requirement from company policy.
- If two sources appear inconsistent, do not reconcile silently; surface the conflict and escalate.
- For employee-specific advice, request/validate establishment type, worker category, headcount, location, wage period and relevant dates.
- For termination, retrenchment, closure, disciplinary action, wage recovery, statutory filing or penalty exposure, provide a compliance checklist and require HR/legal review before execution.
- For scanned rules, inspect the relevant page image when text extraction is incomplete.
